Dewen Guo

Postdoctoral Researcher, University of Utah

I received my Ph.D. from Peking University in 2025 and my B.E. from East China Normal University. My research is on physics-based simulation: numerical optimization and GPU algorithms for deformable bodies, cloth, contact, and multibody dynamics, and their use in robotics and AI.

guodw.sh@gmail.com · LinkedIn · YouTube · Salt Lake City, Utah

Research interests

Physics-based simulation

Elastodynamics with contact, cloth and garments, rods and yarn, peridynamics, affine-body and multibody dynamics.

Optimization and GPU computing

Newton–PCG and augmented-Lagrangian solvers, Jacobi-type and subspace-correction methods, numerical quadrature, matrix-free GPU implementations.

Robotics and AI

Contact models for robot control on GPU; differentiable physics for graphics and learning.
